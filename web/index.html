Record Linkage & Deduplication

Find potential duplicate records in one or two CSV files. Files are processed in this browser tab and are not uploaded to a server.

Local processing

1. Load records

Required columns: first_name, last_name, dob (YYYY-MM-DD). An optional second dataset is combined with the first.

Records are compared only when they share a birth year and first-name initial. This reduces comparisons but can miss some duplicates.

Choose a CSV file or load sample data to begin.

2. Review candidate pairs

Scores use three fields and fixed Fellegi–Sunter probabilities (m = 0.9, u = 0.1). Results are suggestions, not confirmed identities.
